Rowland Heights, CA Rent Prices (2026)

Rowland Heights is one of the most established Chinese-American communities in Southern California, and it offers noticeably more space per dollar than Irvine or Arcadia. As of September 2026, a 3-bedroom rents for roughly $3,600–$3,800 a month and a 4-bedroom house for about $4,300–$4,550.

What renters in Rowland Heights compare

Many renters come to Rowland Heights after comparing it with Irvine, Arcadia or Diamond Bar. The draw is space: the same budget buys a larger home. These renters care about practical things (storage, flooring, a kitchen that works, reliable air conditioning) more than high-end finishes.

Much of Rowland Heights is served by Rowland Unified School District, with Rowland High School a common reference point for families. District boundaries do not follow the community name exactly, so confirm the assigned schools by address.

Pricing tips for landlords

Spend on what solves real problems, such as flooring, storage, kitchen function and cooling, rather than on luxury upgrades that this price-sensitive renter pool will not pay extra for.

Overpricing hurts more here than in Irvine: a listing priced too high often gets almost no showings. If the first two weeks bring few inquiries, adjust the price before peak season passes.
