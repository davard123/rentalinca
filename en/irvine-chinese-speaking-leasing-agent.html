And many Irvine homes are in HOAs that cap the number of rentals or require a minimum lease term — I confirm those rules before listing. Current rent ranges are on the Irvine average rent page.

California rules I walk landlords through

Security deposits are generally capped at one month's rent under AB 12 (Civil Code §1950.5), with a two-month exception for qualifying small landlords; itemized deductions are due within 21 days of move-out. Entry generally needs 24 hours' written notice (§1954). Screening criteria must be given to applicants in writing with the application (§1950.6). AB 1482 rent caps and just-cause rules may apply depending on the property. The full Chinese guide is on the landlord page; for case-specific legal questions, please consult an attorney.
